# Zepto Summer Arcade — Brainstorm Document
> Session: 2026-05-19. Status: Direction locked, mechanic not yet finalized, edge cases mapped.

---

## Strategic Direction

**The core insight:**
Blinkit's "Scream for Ice Cream" succeeded because of: absurd action → immediate confirmation → shareable result. One viral moment. The scream was the hook because it was socially risky — you'd do it in public and that IS the content.

**Zepto's answer is not to create one rival viral moment — it's to create a platform.**

Instead of a standard order tracking screen, a **Zepto Summer Arcade** opens the moment you place a summer order. Active for your delivery window only. Multiple sensor-based mini-games. Each game earns a discount on a summer essential. Every order is a new session, with a new rotation of games.

**Why this beats Blinkit's approach:**
- Blinkit: one trick. Scream once, done.
- Zepto Arcade: every order is a new session. You order *to play*, not just to eat.
- The 10-minute wait becomes anticipated, not tolerated. Wait = gameplay time.
- Multiple viral vectors: any one game can break out independently. The scream had one moment. Zepto Arcade has eight.
- Lives entirely in the 10-minute wait window — Blinkit cannot copy this without contradicting their own speed-first brand promise.
- Platform is reusable for any impulse SKU (biscuits, cold coffee, chocolate) — mechanic outlives summer.

## The 8 Games (Full Descriptions)

### 1. Blow to Beat the Heat
**Sensor:** Microphone
**Action:** A melting ice cream appears on screen. Blow into the mic. The harder/longer you blow, the more it freezes on screen. A "Frost Meter" fills in real time.
**Feedback:** Frost Score out of 100. "Top 12% of blowers today."
**Reward:** Score above threshold = discount on cold drinks (cold coffee, nimbu pani).
**Share card:** "Frost Score: 94 BFU. You vs. 38°C Delhi summer. You won."
**Viral angle:** People blowing on phones in public = the content. Summer-native. City leaderboard (highest frost score in Jaipur, Mumbai, Bangalore).
**Twist:** Daily city leaderboard. Highest frost score per city = bonus reward.

---

### 2. Shake to Select — The Vending Machine
**Sensor:** Accelerometer
**Action:** A vending machine appears. Shake the phone. Intensity of shake (1–10 scale, measured over 3 seconds) determines what drops.
**Tiers:**
- Light (1–3): Nimbu pani, chaas, aam panna, small popsicle
- Medium (4–6): Cornetto, Kwality Walls bar, cold coffee, lassi
- Hard (7–9): Magnum, premium ice cream, fruit beer
- Violent (10): Mystery box — "You broke the machine. Here's everything."
**Reward:** Discount on the dropped item on next order. Or: item added to current cart at a discount.
**Share card:** "Shake intensity: 8.2. Got: Magnum Almond. The machine knew."
**Viral angle:** Slot machine psychology — variable reward = compulsive. "I got nimbu pani, what did you get?" becomes the conversation.
**Twist:** "Break the machine" (score 10) = guaranteed premium item discount. Failure state is funnier than success.

---

### 3. Run the Race
**Sensor:** Pedometer (accelerometer)
**Action:** From order to delivery, track your steps vs. the rider's estimated distance. Two athletes on screen, both counters updating live.
**States:**
- You're winning: "Slow down. You're embarrassing him."
- Rider's winning: "He's pulling ahead. Move."
- Dead heat: "It's a race. Literally."
**Reward:** Out-step the rider = free topping on this order. Rider wins = "Our rider is fitter. Ice cream anyway."
**Share card:** "You: 1,847 steps. Rider: 924 steps. You walked twice as far for ice cream. Commitment." OR "Rider: 1,200. You: 43. You sat completely still. Respect."
**Viral angle:** Both outcomes are shareable. Competitive framing pulls people off the couch. Strava crossover energy.
**Twist:** Weekly "Zepto Runner" leaderboard. Highest cumulative steps across all orders this week = badge + reward.

---

### 4. Tap Race
**Sensor:** Touchscreen
**Action:** Tap the screen as fast as possible for 10 seconds. One large target, center screen. Pure speed.
**Craving Speed tiers:**
- Under 3/sec: "Calm. Suspicious. Are you even hungry?"
- 3–5/sec: "Standard craving. Respectable."
- 5–7/sec: "Now we're talking."
- 7+/sec: "This is a medical emergency. We're hurrying."
**Reward:** Top 10% of today's tappers = discount code. Threshold resets daily.
**Share card:** "362 taps. 7.2/second. Craving classified as: Emergency."
**Viral angle:** Immediate urge to beat own score, then friends' scores. Dinner table game.
**Twist:** Mid-wait friend challenge — share link, friend has 24 hours to beat your score. Both order = both get discount.

---

### 5. Tongue Out Challenge
**Sensor:** Front camera + face detection
**Action:** Virtual ice cream cone appears at mouth level on screen. Stick tongue out and lick toward camera. Each recognized lick = cone shrinks on screen.
**States:**
- 0–10 licks: "Restrained. Are you sure you want ice cream?"
- 10–30 licks: "Getting there."
- 50+: "You licked a phone screen 50 times. This is art."
**Reward:** Top lickers (daily leaderboard) = bonus topping unlocked on next order.
**Share card:** "47 licks before delivery. The ice cream arrived. Then it was licked 48 more times. Mercilessly."
**Viral angle:** Licking a phone screen in public = socially risky = highest share rate. Blinkit scream energy.
**Twist (AR layer):** As you lick, the on-screen ice cream "mixes" flavors based on lick pattern. Your final flavor combo is on the delivery card. Personal + ridiculous.

---

### 6. No Blinking Challenge
**Sensor:** Front camera + face/eye detection
**Action:** Stare-off against a cartoon sun (or melting ice cream). Don't blink for as long as possible. First blink = game over.
**Tiers:**
- Under 10 sec: Bronze — 5% off
- 10–15 sec: Silver — 10% off
- 15–20 sec: Gold — 15% off
- 20+ sec: "Eyes of Steel" badge + max reward
**Share card:** "8.3 seconds. Sun: 0 blinks. You: 1 blink. The sun wins. 10% off anyway." + photo of your blink-face (the exact frame when you blinked).
**Viral angle:** The concentration face + the blink face = comedy. People will video themselves. The failure face IS the content.
**Twist:** The card includes the actual camera frame of your blink moment. That's the screenshot.

---

### 7. Don't Peek
**Sensor:** Proximity sensor + accelerometer
**Action:** Phone goes face down after ordering. App tracks: total face-down duration, number of times you picked it up (peek events).
**States at delivery:**
- 0 peeks: "ICE COLD. You waited without looking once."
- 1–2 peeks: "You peeked. We understand. You're human."
- 3–5 peeks: "You couldn't help yourself. 5 times."
- 6+: "You picked this up 8 times. The ice cream means everything."
**Reward:** 0 peeks = maximum reward (free topping / significant discount). Each peek reduces reward. 6+ peeks = sympathy discount "because you clearly suffered."
**Share card:** "Peek count: 0. Wait time: 8:47. Zero checks. One perfect order." OR "Peek count: 6. Every 90 seconds. Respect the hunger."
**Viral angle:** Sharing peek count like Screen Time stats — in shame or pride. Both cards get posted.
**Twist:** At peek #3, push notification: "We see you. That's peek #3. Last chance for the full reward." Fourth wall broken.

---

### 8. Crowd Amplifier
**Sensor:** Bluetooth (proximity detection)
**Action:** If multiple Zepto users are nearby, orders link. Group challenge unlocks.
**Levels:**
- 2 people: combined tap scores → 2x reward
- 3–4 people (Squad Mode): each picks a challenge, combined results = group discount for all
- 5+ people (Flash Mob): coordinated challenge (e.g., all blow simultaneously for 5 sec)
- City-wide: 10,000+ orders in 1 hour in one city = free topping for everyone in that city
**Share card:** "Mumbai: 12,847 orders in 60 minutes. The city wanted ice cream. The city got ice cream."
**Viral angle:** City-level mechanics generate actual news coverage. Rival cities (Mumbai vs. Delhi) create Twitter India moments.
**Twist:** Rival city leaderboard. Whichever city hits 10K first wins the city unlock. City identity play.

## Rotation System

**Recommended rotation size: 3 games per session**
- Odd number creates psychological incompleteness — harder to walk away
- ~3 minutes of gameplay in a 10-minute window
- "I played all 3" is achievable and satisfying
- Scales to 4 once engagement data supports it

**Rotation pool:** 8 games → 3 shown per session → ~2–3 orders before a repeat set. Right FOMO window.

**Rotation is NOT purely random — context-weighted:**
- Crowd amplifier: only surfaces if 2+ Zepto users are within Bluetooth range
- Blow / tongue out / no blink: only surface on mobile with permissions granted
- Run the race: weights higher evenings (walking context)
- Don't peek: weights higher for users with high peek history (behavioral irony)
- New games can be added to the pool server-side without an app update

**Kill switch:** Any single game can be disabled server-side in minutes. A buggy game never kills the entire arcade.

## Reward Economy

| Game | Reward Category |
|---|---|
| Blow to beat heat | Cold drinks (cold coffee, nimbu pani) |
| Shake vending machine | Dropped item — discount on that category |
| Run the race | Free topping on current order |
| Tap race | Speed discount on next reorder |
| Tongue out | Extra scoop / upgrade |
| No blinking | % off next order |
| Don't peek | Max reward tier — free item potential |
| Crowd amplifier | Group discount, stacks for the whole group |

**Discount cap:** Total game rewards capped at 20% per order regardless of games won. Prevents margin damage.

**Baseline rule (critical):** Every user who opens the arcade gets a baseline 5% reward — regardless of game performance. Games let you earn UP TO 20%. You never lose anything by being bad at a game, you only gain by being good. Games feel like a gift, not a gatekeep.

**Reward expiry:** 72 hours minimum. Countdown visible on reward card.

## Edge Cases — Full Map

### Device & Permissions

| Edge Case | Fix |
|---|---|
| Mic permission denied | Detect before surfacing. Swap blow game out of rotation |
| Camera permission denied | Same — permission check precedes rotation selection |
| No gyroscope (budget Android) | Capability check at session start. Weight rotation away from sensor-heavy games |
| Active phone call during game | Pause game state, resume on call end. No penalty |
| App backgrounded mid-game | Pause. Show "Game paused — come back to finish" |
| No internet mid-game | Store score locally, submit on reconnect. Show optimistic result immediately |
| Low battery (<15%) | Suppress camera games. Show lightweight alternatives |
| User on web app | Show "Games only on Zepto app" card + download link. Never show broken games |

### Sensor-Specific Abuse & Failure

**Blow:**
- Noisy environment registers fake blows → Use directional mic + breath-frequency detection. Reject ambient noise patterns
- Fan directed at phone → Fine. Fun is the goal, not fraud prevention
- Multiple people blowing → Feature. Add co-op blow multiplier

**Shake vending machine:**
- User on bumpy vehicle → Detect GPS speed >15 kmph → disable shake game, swap in non-motion game
- Phone drops mid-shake → Single-frame spike filter: needs 2+ sec sustained motion to count
- Repeated shaking to hit higher tier → Take peak intensity in a 3-second window. One shake, one drop
- Dropped item out of stock → Re-drop a substitute. Never promise unavailable discount

**Run the race:**
- User on wheelchair → Track accelerometer movement, not steps. Rename to "distance covered." Everyone qualifies
- Pedometer permission denied → Fallback: show rider stats only. "Rider covered 1.2km for you." Still shareable
- Rider route changes → Cap rider estimate at 2km. Precision isn't the point

**Tap race:**
- Multi-touch tapping → Allow. You're still physically tapping
- Bot-level auto-tapper → Cap at 15 taps/second (humanly achievable max). Above = invalid

**Tongue out / No blinking:**
- Face detection bias across skin tones → Non-negotiable: use bias-audited model (MediaPipe). Test across full skin tone range before launch. Failure here = PR catastrophe
- Face covering (niqab) → Auto-skip after 5 seconds of no detection. No penalty
- Bad lighting / dark room → Graceful fail, auto-skip

**Don't peek:**
- Emergency call mid-wait → Phone unlocking to phone/messages = grace event (max 2). Unlocking to Zepto app = peek
- Phone in pocket → Proximity + accelerometer stationary = counts as face down. Pocket = valid
- Physical notification buzz → Vibration without pickup ≠ peek. Pickup = peek

**Crowd amplifier:**
- Bluetooth permission denied → Remove from rotation for this device
- Privacy: users must know Bluetooth is being used → Explicit opt-in disclosure before game activates. Not assumed
- Gaming the city challenge → Mass coordination = essentially word-of-mouth marketing. Fine

### Order State

| Scenario | Fix |
|---|---|
| Order cancelled after games | Rewards earned still valid for next order within 24 hours |
| Delivery faster than 10 min | Show remaining games as "bonus round" on delivery confirmation |
| Delivery delayed beyond 10 min | Extend game window to actual delivery time. Delay now has an upside |
| Out-of-stock item mid-wait | Redirect vending machine discount to auto-selected substitute |
| Multiple items in order | Rewards apply to category, not specific SKU |
| User cancels to reorder and farm games | Games unlock once per address per day |

### Discount & Business Logic

| Scenario | Fix |
|---|---|
| All 3 game rewards stacked | Hard cap: 20% max per order total |
| Reward conflicts with promo code | Game rewards = Zepto-funded, auto-apply. Promo codes = brand-funded, apply on top. Different ledgers |
| Min order value not met | Show minimum requirement on reward card at point of earning, not at checkout |
| Margin varies by SKU | All game discounts are percentage-based. Backend sets SKU-level cap |

### UX & Trust

| Scenario | Fix |
|---|---|
| User doesn't know how to play | 5-second animated tutorial per game, skippable. First play only |
| Game sensor failure | "Not working?" tap-out. Auto-skip after 15 sec inactivity. Consolation micro-reward for the trouble |
| Reward doesn't apply at checkout | Rewards visible in cart BEFORE payment. If it's not in cart, it wasn't earned — make this transparent |
| Hindi-speaking users | All game copy in Hindi + English. Toggle. Non-negotiable for Zepto's user base |
| Older / non-game-native users | Games are optional. "Skip to tracking" always visible. Order tracking is always one tap away |

### The Most Critical Edge Case
**Fairness: Don't Peek + No Blinking reward users who have natural advantages** (low anxiety, certain eye conditions). If rewards are significant, users who can't perform the challenge feel excluded.

**Fix:** The baseline reward (5% off) goes to everyone who opens the arcade. Games only earn additional reward, never gate the baseline. Games feel like a bonus, not a requirement.
